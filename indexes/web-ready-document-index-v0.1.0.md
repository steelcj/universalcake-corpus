## Website Pages

### Landing Page

**File:** `website/landing-page-v0.1.0.md`

The front door to Universal Cake. Covers the problem, the seven dimensions, the approach (accessibility-first, language as infrastructure, edge innovation, sovereignty), the origin story, guiding questions, and the core shift. Designed to stand alone as a website landing page.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**File:** `website/faq-v0.1.0.md`

Twelve questions covering what Universal Cake is, how it differs from WCAG, what "accessibility first" and "language is infrastructure" mean, who it is for, why sovereignty matters for accessibility, the 80/20 lie, the Virtuous Tornado, the rebuildability test, how to use the framework, where the name came from, and licensing.

### Glossary of Key Concepts

**File:** `website/glossary-v0.1.0.md`

Twenty-five defined terms spanning the full framework: from accessibility, ecotone effect, and Virtuous Tornado through the convenience trap, capability erosion, sovereignty dividend, and cascade priority. Each entry provides a concise definition grounded in the source material and cited research.

### Self-Assessment Checklist

**File:** `website/self-assessment-checklist-v0.1.0.md`

A practical tool for organisations to evaluate their own technology stack across all seven Universal Cake dimensions plus a sovereignty assessment. Each dimension includes actionable questions and a collapse signal — the indicator that the dimension needs attention before others can improve.

## Standalone Articles

### The 80/20 Lie

**File:** `articles/the-80-20-lie-v0.1.0.md`

*How a Rule About Italian Land Ownership Became a Licence to Ignore Most of Humanity.* The Pareto misapplication, the actual numbers on language and exclusion, compounded digital exclusion, the measurable cost, and the counter-case that innovation lives at the edge. The single most shareable piece in the corpus.

### You Don't Own What You Can't Rebuild

**File:** `articles/you-dont-own-what-you-cant-rebuild-v0.1.0.md`

The digital sovereignty argument distilled to article length. The four erosion loops (convenience trap, abstraction spiral, capability erosion, market concentration), the delays that hide damage, the system traps, leverage points ranked from parameters to paradigms, and the sovereignty dividend. Centred on the rebuildability test as a single actionable governance gate.

### Innovation Lives at the Edge

**File:** `articles/innovation-lives-at-the-edge-v0.1.0.md`

The ecological and design-research case for edge-first design. The ecotone effect (Odum), the Virtuous Tornado (Treviranus), bridging capital (Putnam/March), the Plone evidence from 1999, and the honest two-direction argument — the harm of exclusion is real and the resilience it produces is also real. Neither cancels the other.

### Language Is Infrastructure

**File:** `articles/language-is-infrastructure-v0.1.0.md`

The argument that language is load-bearing, not cosmetic. The reinforcing loop in both directions, what weak language infrastructure produces, language as the first accessibility gate, language and sovereignty, the Quebec context, and what building on language infrastructure looks like in practice.

### The Cascade Priority

**File:** `articles/the-cascade-priority-v0.1.0.md`

*What to Build First, and Why.* The natural startup sequence derived from systems thinking: protect human capacity first, then open participation, then build knowledge. Why skipping a step collapses the cascade. Co-reinforcement between loops and the conditions for the Virtuous Tornado.
