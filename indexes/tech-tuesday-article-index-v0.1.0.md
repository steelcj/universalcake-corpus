## About the Newsletter

*Tech Tuesday with Qvelo* is a weekly LinkedIn newsletter published by Qvelo Corporation. It covers real-world AI, HPC, and data system deployments with a focus on infrastructure architecture, sovereign compute, and practical implementation. Where Roy Chartier's *State of Mind* addresses governance and strategy, Tech Tuesday addresses the technical layer beneath those decisions: hardware, software stacks, identity systems, storage, and control planes.

- **Newsletter archive:** [linkedin.com/newsletters/tech-tuesday-with-qvelo-7347763956955963392](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/tech-tuesday-with-qvelo-7347763956955963392/)
- **Publisher profile:** [linkedin.com/company/qvelo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qvelo/)

> Note: LinkedIn requires a logged-in account to access full article content. All links above go to publicly listed pages; some may require login to read.

> Note on numbering: Two articles in the feed both carry the label Issue 28. This appears to be a numbering error in the original publications. Both are listed here as published. Issues 3 through 7 carry no visible issue numbers in their titles; numbers are inferred from their position in the feed sequence. The Architecture Methodology Series (issues listed at the end) uses a new series label rather than continuing the main issue number sequence.
