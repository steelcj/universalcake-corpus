## Number formatting note (footnote)

In Québec’s French/European financial notation:  
- A **space** is the thousands separator.  
- A **comma (,)** is the decimal separator.  

Thus:  
- **134 526,00** = **$134,526.00 CAD** (one hundred thirty-four thousand),  
- not 134 million (which would appear as **134 000 000,00**).  

This clarification prevents misreading the official ledger figures.
